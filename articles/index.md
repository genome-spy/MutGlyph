# Articles

### Get started

- [Get started with interactive cancer genomics in
  R](https://genomespy.app/MutGlyph/articles/MutGlyph.md):

  Create an interactive oncoplot in R from a maftools MAF object and
  explore the zooming, panning, tooltips, and export controls provided
  by MutGlyph.

### Plot guides

- [Interactive oncoplots in
  R](https://genomespy.app/MutGlyph/articles/oncoplots.md):

  Create interactive, maftools-compatible oncoplots in R with zooming,
  tooltips, clinical annotations, TiTv tracks, summaries, and GISTIC
  calls.

- [Interactive protein lollipop plots in
  R](https://genomespy.app/MutGlyph/articles/lollipop-plots.md):

  Explore protein mutations with interactive lollipop plots in R using
  MAF objects or data frames, custom domains, labels, and cohort
  comparisons.

- [Interactive, zoomable rainfall plots and kataegis in
  R](https://genomespy.app/MutGlyph/articles/rainfall-plots.md):

  Explore mutation spacing with interactive rainfall plots in R, zoom
  into dense genomic regions, and highlight candidate kataegis loci.

- [Interactive, zoomable GISTIC copy-number landscapes in
  R](https://genomespy.app/MutGlyph/articles/gistic-plots.md):

  Explore GISTIC amplification and deletion peaks with an interactive,
  zoomable copy-number landscape in R, including significance and
  annotations.
